# Fair Play and Acceptable Use Policy

**BlunderProof is a chess training tool. It is not, and may never be used as, a source of outside assistance during a game of chess.**

This policy applies to everyone who accesses or uses BlunderProof in any form, including the website, any hosted service, the source code in this repository, and any copy, fork, build, or derivative work of it (together, "BlunderProof"). By accessing or using BlunderProof you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, do not use BlunderProof.

## 1. Prohibited use

You may not use BlunderProof, or any output, analysis, suggestion, annotation, or feature of it, to:

1. Get assistance of any kind during a game of chess that is in progress. This covers rated and unrated games, casual and tournament games, online and over-the-board games, and correspondence and daily games wherever the rules of that game or platform forbid outside assistance.
2. Violate the fair play rules, terms of service, or code of conduct of any chess platform, club, federation, league, or event. This includes, without limitation, Chess.com, Lichess, Chess24, FIDE, national federations, and any other online or over-the-board competition.
3. Analyze, evaluate, or suggest moves for a position from a game that is still in progress, whether the position is entered by hand, imported, scraped, screen-captured, relayed by another person, or obtained by any other means.
4. Build, enable, or support any tool, bot, extension, overlay, relay, or automation that gives a player outside assistance during a game, or that hides or obscures the use of such assistance.
5. Help, encourage, or enable any other person to do any of the above.

## 2. Intended use

BlunderProof is meant only for study and practice away from live competition. Examples include training exercises, reviewing your own games after they have finished, and play against the tool itself when no other game is in progress.
